# The Reality First

A true autonomous aircraft system needs to solve:

1. Perception
   “What am I seeing?”

2. Localization
   “Where am I?”

3. Mapping
   “What does the world look like?”

4. Planning
   “Where should I fly?”

5. Control
   “How do I move safely?”

6. Decision making
   “What if something unexpected happens?”

7. Safety redundancy
   “What if sensors fail?”

This is why companies spend billions.

So your best strategy is:

# Start with autonomous drones first

NOT full passenger aircraft.

That’s how almost every serious autonomy company started.
